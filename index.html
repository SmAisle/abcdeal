How to Play

  • Goal: beat the dealer in today's 5 hands. Everyone gets the same cards, and so does the dealer, so you can compare scores.
  • Spell a word: tap your cards to fill the slots, then tap Play word. Hand 1 needs 3 letters, then 4, 5, 6 and 7 letters in hand 5.
  • Cards: 5 in hand 1, one more each hand, up to 9. The dealer gets the same number and shows you one card. There are always at least 4 common words to find (2 in hand 5), and your best word always beats the dealer's.
  • Points: add up the numbers on your cards. Your first letter counts ×1 in hand 1, ×2 in hand 2, up to ×5 in hand 5.
    1: A E I L N O R S T U · 2: D G · 3: B C M P
    4: F H V W Y · 5: K · 8: J X · 10: Q Z
  • The dealer flips its cards and plays its best word. Higher score wins the hand; a tie is a push. No word for the dealer? It busts and you win.
  • Stuck? Can't find a word? Tap Fold to give up the hand.
  • Difficulty: a careful robot plays each deal ahead of time and rates it from Easy to Hard, like The Daily 15. You see it when you finish.
  • Stars: win hands to earn them.

  • 💯 Perfect: play the best word in every hand to score every possible point.
  • Deal ’Em (Plus): a second deal every day, played like Texas Hold’em. You and the dealer share the board in the middle and each get 2 cards of your own. Your word has to use at least one of your 2 cards.
  • 🔥 Streak: finish each day's deal on its day to build your streak.
  • The website version sends an anonymous check-in so we can count players: the day, hands played and the game version. Never your name, contacts or location.

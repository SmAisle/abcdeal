How to Play

  • Goal: beat the dealer in today's 5 hands. Everyone gets the same cards, and so does the dealer.
  • Spell a word: tap your cards to fill the slots, then tap Play word. Drag a card to any slot to start from the end or the middle, onto another card to swap them, or tap it to take it back. Hand 1 needs 3 letters, up to 7 letters in hand 5.
  • Cards: 5 in hand 1, one more each hand, up to 9. The dealer shows you one card. There are always words to find, and your best word always beats the dealer's.
  • Points: add up the numbers on your cards. One spot in each word is the bonus: its letter counts ×2 in hand 2, up to ×5 in hand 5. The bonus spot moves every hand.
    1: A E I L N O R S T U · 2: D G · 3: B C M P
    4: F H V W Y · 5: K · 8: J X · 10: Q Z
  • The dealer flips its cards and plays its best word. Higher score wins; a tie is a push. No word? The dealer busts.
  • Stars: win hands to earn them.

    Stars⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  • 🃏 A, B, C, Deal ’Em: a second deal every day, played like Texas Hold’em. You and the dealer share the board in the middle and each get 2 cards of your own. Your word must use at least one of them.
  • 🏳️ Fold: can't find a word? You get 2 💡 hints a day: the Fold button is a Hint button until you use them. Pick any slot and see its letter; it locks there for the hand. Each hint costs one star. After that the button is Fold. Or give up the hand and see a word you could have played.
  • 💯 Perfect: play the best word in every hand to score every possible point.
  • 📊 Difficulty: a careful robot plays each deal ahead of time and rates it from Easy to Hard.
  • 🔥 Streak: finish each day's deal on its day to build your streak. Archive deals played late don't count.
  • 🗂️ Archive: missed a day? Play any deal back to Sept 1 from the front page or the menu.
  • 🔁 Play again: replay today's deal for fun after you finish. It doesn't count.
  • The website version sends an anonymous check-in so we can count players: the day, hands played and the game version. Never your name, contacts or location.

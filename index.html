A, B, C, Deal

Terms

  • A, B, C, Deal is a free game, just for fun. It may change or stop working at any time.
  • No accounts and no ads. Nothing is sold.
  • Your scores and streak stay on your device.
  • The website version sends an anonymous check-in when you open the game and finish a hand, so we can count how many devices play each day: the game version, hands played and won, and your best day. It never includes your name, contacts or location.

By tapping Agree and play, you accept these terms.

How to play

Beat the dealer with a better word. Everyone gets the same five hands each day.

  • Each hand needs a longer word: 3 letters in hand 1, then 4, 5, 6 and 7 letters in hand 5. The dealer plays by the same rule.
  • Every hand is a fresh deal: 5 cards in hand 1, one more each hand, up to 9 in hand 5. The dealer gets the same number. Everyone gets the same cards each day, and there is always at least one word to find.
  • Each card is worth its points, like Scrabble. Tap cards to spell your word, then tap Play word.
  • Under your word, the game counts how many common words your cards can make. After each hand it tells you what the best word was worth.
  • The first letter of your word counts extra: ×1 in hand 1, ×2 in hand 2, up to ×5 in hand 5. Start with a big letter! The dealer's first letter counts the same way.
  • No word possible? You bust: you lose the hand and throw away your cards. Can't find a word that's there? Tap Fold to give up the hand the same way.
  • The dealer shows one card. When you play, it flips the rest and plays its best word. Higher score wins; a tie is a push. If the dealer has no word, the dealer busts and you win.

Example, hand 5

In hand 5, S·H·A·V·E = 1+4+1+4+1 = 11, and the S counts ×5, so 15. Rearranged as H·A·V·E·S, the H counts ×5: 11 + 16 = 27.
